## Accessibility and review

- [ ] The diagram has a clear start and direction.
- [ ] The text conveys all information shown in the diagram.
- [ ] Labels are readable and do not depend on color.
- [ ] The Mermaid source is kept in the repository.
- [ ] A reviewer can explain the diagram's purpose and intended reading.
- [ ] The diagram contains no real patient, beneficiary, staff, or confidential data.
