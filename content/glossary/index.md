This glossary explains terms used in ZarishHealth documentation. Definitions describe the proposed design unless a page says otherwise; they do not imply that a feature has been implemented.

## Browse by topic

- [Core platform terms](core-platform-terms.md): organization, local-first, API, and software workspace.
- [Healthcare standards](healthcare-standards.md): FHIR, profiles, terminology, and synthetic data.
- [Governance and operations](governance-operations.md): proposals, architecture decisions, and data responsibility.
- [Development and deployment](development-deployment.md): source repositories, CI, and deployment terms.
- [Data and integration](data-integration.md): identifiers, offline synchronization, privacy, and security concepts.

## How definitions are written

Definitions use plain language first, then explain the ZarishHealth context and link to the authoritative source. Where the blueprint leaves a question open, the glossary says so.
