## How to read status labels

Every page labels the maturity of its information. **Proposed** means a suggestion that still needs review. **Adopted** means maintainers have approved the guidance. **Implemented** means someone has verified that the described behavior exists. Do not treat a proposal as an implemented feature.

## Current repository inventory

The organization currently has four repositories: [`.github`](https://github.com/zarishhealth/.github), [`docs`](https://github.com/zarishhealth/docs), [`builder`](https://github.com/zarishhealth/builder), and [`zarishhealth.github.io`](https://github.com/zarishhealth/zarishhealth.github.io). The [repository map](architecture/repository-map.md) separates this current inventory from future repositories proposed in the blueprint.

## Safety and privacy

This repository contains documentation, not patient records. Use synthetic examples only. Do not put personal health information, credentials, or confidential deployment details in pages, diagrams, issues, or pull requests. The documentation does not certify legal or regulatory compliance.
